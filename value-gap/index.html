Read the gap

Each player has three aligned rows: current production, your ROS projection, and the difference (current − projection).

Green means better fantasy performance; red means worse. A negative turnover difference is green when turnovers have a negative scoring weight.

Both FPTS rows use the same league scoring. The lists are ordered by the gap in FPTS per game; a negative gap suggests a buy-low candidate and a positive gap a sell-high candidate, assuming your projections are the baseline.

Missing stats show “—”. Players missing any stat with a nonzero scoring weight cannot be ranked. Unmatched names and incomplete players are reported above the lists. Recent windows mean games played, not calendar days.

These gaps measure production against an assumed baseline. They don’t measure an opponent’s trade price or guarantee regression. Use current, pure ROS projections; BLEND sources incorporate recent production and reduce independence.
